ABOUT THE NGO

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation was established on September 23, 2020 and operates as a Section 8 registered NGO. The organization focuses on supporting underprivileged communities through education, food distribution, environmental conservation, women empowerment, animal welfare, and youth development programs.

Recognized & Certified

80G & 12A Certified, CSR‑1 Registered, NITI Aayog Registered and ISO 9001:2015 Certified.

Volunteer Driven

Supported by interns, volunteers, professionals, and community partners across India.

Community Focused

Every initiative is designed to create practical and sustainable social impact.
